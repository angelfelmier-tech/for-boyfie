A letter for you

Happy First Monthsary, Maung.

I still can't believe it's been a month already. I don't think i'll ever get used to being able to call you my boyfriend. If someone had told my past self that I’d end up here, being your girlfriend, I think she would’ve had a hard time believing it. Love has always felt like something you have to be careful with, something that could leave you regretting how much of yourself you gave away. But with you, I haven’t felt the need to keep one foot out the door. I’ve just wanted to see where this goes, and I’m glad I let myself.

That's probably one of the things I appreciate most about our relationship. You've made love feel less like a test I have to pass and more like a language I’m finally getting comfortable speaking. I’m still learning, and I know I don’t always know how to explain what I feel, but I like that I get to learn with you. I like that I don’t have to have the perfect words every time for what I feel to be real. I’m getting used to the way you fit into my days, too. How you’ve become someone I think about when I wake up, someone I want to tell things to, even when the things themselves aren’t particularly interesting. Sometimes I’ll hear something or see something and immediately think of you, and I wonder when my mind started making that connection so naturally.

And I love the parts of you that you probably don't think much about. The way your words sometimes tumble over each other when you’re excited, the changes in your voice when you’re sleepy, the way I can tell there’s more to what you’re feeling than you always let on. I like getting to know you beyond what you say directly. You’re not a puzzle I need to solve, Adan. I just want to keep paying attention, because there’s always something about you worth knowing a little better.

Of course, I wish the distance would be a little kinder to us. There are days when I wish I could be beside you instead of having to imagine what it would be like to hold your hand, hear your laugh in the same room, or give you a kiss without a screen between us. I want to experience the things we can only talk about for now. I want to know what it’s like to share the same space with you, to look at you while you’re talking instead of looking at a screen, to be there for the things that don’t make it into our calls.

But I've also come to appreciate what we have right now. We're building something with circumstances that don’t always make it easy, and yet you’ve never made me feel like I’m waiting for my turn to be loved. You make room for me in the life you have, and I hope you feel that I’m trying to do the same for you. I know we can’t give each other everything we want just yet, but I’m grateful that we can still give each other something real.

Thank you for being patient with me, for being honest with me, and for giving me room to be myself around you. There are parts of me that take time to show, and I’m glad I don’t feel like I have to force them out just to prove that I trust you. There's really still so much of me you haven't seen, and I want you to be the one who gets to know those parts too.

I hope you know that I want to be that kind of person for you, too. Someone you can be honest with even when the truth is inconvenient, someone who will listen when you have a lot to say and when you don’t quite know how to say anything at all. I don’t want us to be good at loving each other only when everything is easy. I want us to learn how to be kind to each other when we’re tired, confused, or having a day when neither of us quite feels like ourselves.

And I know one month isn’t that long, and there’s still so much about each other we haven’t figured out yet. But I’m happy with what we have, and I love getting to know you more every day. You’ve become someone I find myself thinking about even when we’re doing completely different things. I catch myself picking up your words and the way you talk, then suddenly I’ll be doing it at school and giggling to myself while my friends wonder what’s so funny. It’s embarrassing how you manage to make me smile without even being there. There’s still so much about you I haven’t gotten to know, and I’m excited to keep learning, without either of us having to be anyone other than ourselves.

You’ve become part of the way I see the world, Adan. You’re my moon, my sun, my stars, my whole galaxy, and I know that sounds like I’m giving you the entire universe, but I don’t know how else to explain it. You find your way into my thoughts before the day has even properly begun, and you’ve become part of the future I catch myself imagining. I want you in ways that distance makes difficult to explain. I want your presence, your voice beside me instead of coming through a speaker, your hand in mine, and the chance to look at you and know I don’t have to say anything for you to understand how happy I am to be there with you. I need you in my life because, out of all the things I look forward to, sharing more of this life with you is one of the things I want most. If the universe gave me a thousand skies to look at, I think I would still search for the one that reminds me of you. And if I had to learn a thousand different ways to say I love you, I would, because even these words feel too small for everything I hold for you. I hope that one day, when I finally get to stand in front of you, I can look into your eyes and let you see what all these letters have been trying to tell you. Out of all the people I could have met and all the lives I could have lived, I am so unbelievably grateful that mine led me to you.

Happy first month, my baobei. I’m really happy it’s you. Thank you for choosing me, and for letting me choose you, too. I can’t promise that I’ll always know the right thing to say or that I’ll never get scared, but I can promise that what I feel for you is something I want to take care of. I want to keep showing up for us, to keep learning you, and to give this relationship the chance to become everything we’re willing to build together.

I hope this is the first of many months we get to celebrate. And when we finally get to be in the same place, I hope I get to tell you all of this again without having to type a single word.
